Questions, answered

Is this a final credit decision?

No. It is an automated estimate from a machine learning model and does not replace a bank's official decision.

Which model is used?

A logistic regression pipeline trained on historical loan outcomes.

What details do I need?

Basic personal information, employment, income and savings, credit score, existing loans, debt-to-income ratio and your loan request.

How long does it take?

Filling the form takes a minute or two. The result itself appears instantly after you submit.

Ready to see where you stand?

Takes about a minute. No signup required.
